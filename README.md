# 🪔 Virtual Diwali Crackers 🎆

Celebrate Diwali with colourful fireworks and virtual crackers! Enjoy the festive animations, add your own greeting, and share the joy of Diwali with family and friends — all without using real crackers.

## ✨ How to Use

1. **Choose a cracker:** Select any cracker from the options at the bottom of the screen.
2. **Tap to celebrate:** Tap anywhere in the display to light your selected cracker and enjoy the animation.
3. **Send a greeting:** Enter your name or a short Diwali message in the message box and tap **🚀 Send**. Your message will appear in the sky with a rocket effect.
4. **Enjoy the finale:** Tap **✨** at the top to launch a special fireworks show.
5. **Control the sound:** Tap **🔊** to switch sound on or off. Quiet mode also turns off vibration.
6. **Share a picture:** Tap **📸** to share a picture of your celebration. If sharing isn't available on your device, the picture will be downloaded instead.

Celebrate with virtual fireworks instead of real crackers. Enjoy the festive fun without physical fireworks, smoke, or cracker waste.
